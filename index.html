Why launch all three

The work becomes a reusable method.

The Method defines how construction-grade plans are made and executed. The influx.avi plan applies it to one system. The release build will follow that plan from the first dependency to final proof. Releasing all three makes the intent, capability boundaries, build order, and proof standard inspectable on day one.

Independent builders can use the plan to create differently named implementations with their own agents and hardware. Those versions will not be official influx.avi releases, and design portability does not prove a working or supported port.

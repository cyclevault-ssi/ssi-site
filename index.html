Talk to your recordings.

Influx turns audio recordings and documents into transcripts and summaries, then lets a local agent discuss them with you. Free for personal use, with the source open to read. Built for Apple Silicon Macs.

Get notified at launch

What Influx does

Transcription

Convert recordings into searchable text.

Summaries

Get the short version of a long recording or document.

Document import

Bring in text documents alongside your recordings.

Local agent

Discuss your recordings and transcripts with an agent that runs on your Mac, powered by Granite.
